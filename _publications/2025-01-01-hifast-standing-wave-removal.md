Standing waves from the reflections inside the FAST feed and receiver produce sinusoidal ripples in the spectra of single-dish 21 cm observations and can masquerade as, or bury, real HI emission. This paper presents the standing-wave removal method implemented in the HiFAST pipeline and quantifies its performance on FAST HI spectral-line data.
