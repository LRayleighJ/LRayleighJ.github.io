HiFAST is the calibration and imaging pipeline for HI spectral-line data taken with the FAST 19-beam receiver. It covers the full path from raw data to science-ready spectra and maps: RFI flagging, flux and baseline calibration, standing-wave removal, and gridding/imaging. It is the pipeline used for the FAST HD² Survey and the FAST × DESI intensity mapping analysis.
