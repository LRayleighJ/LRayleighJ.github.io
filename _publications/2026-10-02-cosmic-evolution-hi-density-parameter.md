Tracing neutral hydrogen across cosmic time in IllustrisTNG-100 with the Krumholz (2013) partitioning model, deriving the HI density parameter and its dependence on cosmic-web environment, and assessing what the environmental distribution of HI implies for 21 cm stacking measurements.
