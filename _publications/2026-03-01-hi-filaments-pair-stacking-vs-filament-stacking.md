Diffuse HI in the filaments of the cosmic web is far below the detection threshold of any single-dish 21 cm observation, so it must be recovered statistically by stacking. This paper compares two approaches — stacking on galaxy pairs and stacking along reconstructed filaments — for detecting HI filaments, and assesses what each method recovers of the cosmic HI budget.
