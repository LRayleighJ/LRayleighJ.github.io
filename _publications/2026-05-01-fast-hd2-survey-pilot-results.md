The FAST Hundred-deg² HI Deep (HD²) Survey is a deep HI 21 cm survey of the HD2 deep field, observed with the FAST 19-beam receiver. This paper presents the early results from the pilot survey, including the survey design, data reduction with the HiFAST pipeline, and the resulting HI catalogue and data products. The HD2 field is the same field used for the FAST × DESI intensity mapping cross-correlation analysis.
