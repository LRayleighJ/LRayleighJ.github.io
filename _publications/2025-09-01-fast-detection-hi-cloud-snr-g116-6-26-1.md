Supernova remnants sweep up and interact with the neutral interstellar medium, but the associated HI is usually faint and easily confused with foreground emission. Using FAST observations, this paper reports the detection of an HI cloud around the supernova remnant G116.6−26.1, and discusses its association with the remnant and its implications for the local ISM.
