**First-author project, in preparation.** Results are not yet public.

Cross-correlating FAST HI intensity mapping with DESI DR1 galaxy spectroscopy in the HD2 deep field, in order to constrain the cosmic HI abundance and its halo-model decomposition. The analysis is built end to end on the HiFAST pipeline reduction, foreground subtraction, and the construction of inverse-variance-weighted HI and galaxy fields, and is validated with a suite of null tests.
